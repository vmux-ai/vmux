# Cook dinner

You have chickpeas, spinach, and rice, and want dinner for two in about 25 minutes. Use a Session to turn that into a recipe, a short shopping list, and something you can make again.

## 1. Start with what you have

Open a recipe site or start a Session:

> Find a vegetarian dinner for two using chickpeas and spinach, ready in about 25 minutes. I also have rice. Show a recipe source, what else I need, and a practical order for the steps.

Tell the agent your available equipment, dietary requirements, and ingredients. Ask it to read your food-preferences note in Knowledge if those details are already saved there.

## 2. Choose a recipe you can follow

Keep the recipe page open beside the conversation. Ask the agent to explain unfamiliar steps, scale the ingredients, or suggest a substitution for something you lack. Check that the plan still fits your time and equipment.

For a whole week's meals, use a Project with separate Sessions for meal ideas and shopping. Share the accepted menu with the shopping Session so it uses the same portions and ingredients.

## 3. Fill the gaps

Ask for a shopping list containing only the missing items. Review the quantities. If you want to order groceries, open the retailer in Browser and follow [Buy something](shopping.md) through the basket and checkout.

## 4. Cook and keep the useful changes

Keep the recipe and step order visible while cooking. Follow up in the same Session when you need a clarification.

Afterwards, tell the agent what you changed and what worked. Ask it to save the recipe source, quantities, and your changes in Knowledge. Bookmark the original recipe under a Recipes folder. Next time, ask an agent to find that saved version.

See [Knowledge](knowledge.md), [Browser](browser.md), and [Agents](agents.md).
